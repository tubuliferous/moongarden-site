Save to MoonGarden: Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

Save to MoonGarden is a browser extension that saves the page you are reading to the MoonGarden app on your own computer.

What the extension handles

When you click the extension's button or press its shortcut, and only then, it reads the address and title of the tab you are on and hands them to the MoonGarden app running on the same computer.

Where that goes

Nowhere else. The address and title are passed directly to the MoonGarden app on your computer. The extension has no server, no account and no analytics. It does not store anything itself, and it does not send anything to the developer or to any third party.

What it does not do

  • It does not read pages you have not asked it to save.
  • It does not track your browsing or build a history.
  • It does not sell, share or transfer any data.

The MoonGarden app

Pages you save are kept by the MoonGarden app in a library on your own computer. To save a page, the app fetches it from the website it came from, as your browser would.
